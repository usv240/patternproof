# PatternProof — 2:50 demo script

The rules require a 1–3 minute public video, explicit explanation of the YouCam API, and footage of the working product. Keep the final cut at or below 2:50 so platform title cards cannot push it across 3:00.

## Before recording

- Use only the rights-cleared fixtures documented in `ASSETS.md`.
- Use a freshly rotated YouCam credential; show no dashboards, environment panels, terminals, email addresses, tokens, or signed URLs.
- Record the product at the final HTTPS origin in a fresh browser profile.
- Pre-create one editable brief and one approved Cut Card as recovery paths.
- Capture the full path once cold and once warm before filming.
- Show no third-party logos or branded imagery; use no copyrighted music, customer information, or unverified impact statistics. Plain-text API identification remains required.

## Timeline and narration

### 0:00–0:15 — the irreversible moment

**Picture:** close tabletop shot: plain fabric, chalk, tape, and scissors. No logos. The scissors stop just above the fabric.

**Narration:**

> “Most AI try-on helps someone decide what to buy. But made-to-order clothing has a different moment of risk: two people must agree on what to make before the first irreversible cut.”

### 0:15–0:38 — audience and problem

**Picture:** landing page, then a phone holding a garment inspiration beside the fabric. Show only the aggregate research counts—never review text, people, or business identities.

**Narration:**

> “India has about 12 million custom tailors—99 percent informal and 72 percent women. In our formative coding of 108 low-star public complaints, 24 described expectation mismatch and eight more alleged damaged customer fabric. This is not a prevalence estimate or product validation; it is why a clear pre-cut agreement deserves testing.”

**On-screen source footer:** `PLFS 2023–24 analysis: Data For India · formative manual coding: RESEARCH.md`

### 0:38–0:59 — private intake and quality gate

**Picture:** sign in; start a Cut Card; select the synthetic customer and garment images; show consent and rights confirmations. Briefly select the poor body fixture and show the local rejection, then restore the clean fixture.

**Narration:**

> “PatternProof starts with a private customer photo and a rights-cleared inspiration image. It strips metadata, normalizes both files, records their proofs, and rejects poor light or framing before a billable YouCam request.”

### 0:59–1:22 — YouCam moment

**Picture:** generate the preview and reveal the recorded olive-dress result from the live YouCam flow. Keep the disclaimer visible.

**Narration:**

> “Server-side YouCam Clothes Virtual Try-On V3 turns that garment reference into a body-specific visual-intent preview. This is not a fit or fabric-drape guarantee. It creates a shared visual for a construction conversation.”

**On-screen:** `YouCam Clothes VTO V3 · server-only credential · private persisted result`

### 1:22–1:50 — the human feasibility layer

**Picture:** mark one item “Can make as shown”; mark one “with adjustment” and enter the trim note. Pin each decision onto the exact visual detail. Briefly mark one “Not feasible” so its pin turns red and the approval gate blocks; resolve it to show the Agreement Map become share-ready.

**Narration:**

> “The customer requested a high square neckline, but the preview is wrap-style. The model cannot promise what a tailor can construct, so every non-negotiable needs a human decision. An adjustment must be explained, and each decision is pinned to the exact visual detail. One not-feasible item turns red and blocks review. This Agreement Map is where a try-on becomes a production workflow.”

### 1:50–2:18 — customer veto and Revision Replay

**Picture:** create the private review; switch to an incognito customer window; show the exact proof and mapped decisions. Enter “Raise the neckline” and request a new revision. Return to the tailor view: show **CHANGE REQUESTED — CUTTING BLOCKED**, accept the request, and reveal Revision Replay from V1 to V2.

**Narration:**

> “The customer does not have to accept a persuasive image. They can veto this exact frozen proof. That request wins a database race against approval, blocks cutting, and becomes a traceable version two instead of an edited history.”
### 2:18–2:38 — consent to cut and production proof

**Picture:** complete V2; show the Cut Readiness Passport move to **6/6 CUT READY** as the customer approves. Montage the locally generated QR, print action, WhatsApp handoff, body-photo erasure, and `/proof` byte-identical repeat evidence.

**Narration:**

> “Only six conditions produce Cut Ready: rights, preview, human decisions, feasibility, frozen proof, and customer approval. Then the record can travel by print or WhatsApp, and the body photo can be erased while the agreement remains.”
### 2:38–2:50 — money shot and honest impact

**Picture:** put the approved Cut Card on the phone beside the fabric. The scissors lift; cut to logo before they touch.

**Narration:**

> “We do not promise fewer remakes. A pilot would measure late changes. PatternProof is the agreement before the cut.”

**End card:** `PatternProof · Agree before the cut · Built with YouCam Clothes VTO V3`

## Editing acceptance

- Runtime at or below 2:50.
- Working product footage, not mockups, occupies at least 90 seconds.
- YouCam API is named verbally and on screen.
- The poor-input and not-feasible states are visible.
- Preview disclaimer is legible.
- Research appears only as aggregate counts with the purposive-sample caveat; no review text or business identity appears.
- All text remains readable on a phone-sized video player.
- No secret, raw bearer link, personal data, brand mark, or unlicensed audio appears in any frame.
